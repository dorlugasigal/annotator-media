Rhythm: SLAM-slam-slam-DIVE | slow glide | hold-push | whip | snap | zoom-through | click-walk | follow-the-click | blur-to-night | lock-hold.
One monolithic `index.html`; `tools/make_phone.py` generates `phone.html` for the portrait render.
Beat numbers below are reel beats. Shot beats (`k`) in `tools/shots.py` count from the shot's first beat.
Music: "Product Launch Advertising Commercial Music" (lNPLUSMUSIC, Pixabay) at its own 130 BPM. The build's last
beat plays under the dive, the second drop hits "Draw", one 2-bar edit hides under the zoom-through (beat 42) and
the final hit lands on the end card (beat 58). Foley (`tools/build_audio.py`): clicks, keys and pen strokes for
actions on screen.

## Beats 1-4 — Hook: Draw / over / any app.

Frame 0 is the final end card. The camera dives into the apricot ellipse round
"any app." while an apricot iris grows (beat 1). "Draw" slams on apricot with a
navy underline (beat 2). "over" snaps in on periwinkle under an arcing arrow
(beat 3). "any app." is cut out of the night with the real dashboard behind it,
then the camera pushes through the "p" (beat 4).

## Beats 4-11 — Toolbar tour

Dashboard take, `k` 0-8. The camera dives from the wide dashboard onto the real
toolbar and glides slowly from Draw and Desk to Select. Labels pinned to the
footage draw in on half beats: Tool library, Effects, "Pen, text and
highlighter", "Arrows and lines", Shapes. They fade as the pointer arrives and
chooses Ellipse on beat 12.

## Beats 12-29 — Marks

Dashboard take, `k` 8-26.
- Crane up to the KPI; the rough teal ellipse is drawn. "Circle it."
- Back to the toolbar: Arrow, then Stroke color. The camera centres on the
  colour picker; the pointer rests on Blue, then chooses it.
- The arrow's three points land: "Point at it." The middle point is dragged:
  "Bend it." bends its own arrow with the real drag.
- A short pause, a hover, then Highlighter; the note is highlighted and
  "Highlight it." gets the same pass.

## Beat 30 — Cut 1: whip

The camera whips left out of the notes and into the design review; the caption
flies out with a directional blur and an apricot swoosh crosses the cut.

## Beats 30-41 — Smart Draw

Smart take, `k` 0-12, all marks in the Sunset gradient. "Draw it rough." flies
in with the whip; a scribble draws with the real pen stroke round Pay now. On
the real snap the words become "It snaps clean." and the scribble morphs into a
clean box. A rough arrow snaps clean. "Add a note." grows like a text box as
the real label "Make it bigger" is typed.

## Beat 42 — Cut 2: zoom-through

The camera and the caption rush into the label; a periwinkle ring and a short
flash open onto the next take, which starts zoomed into the toolbar.

## Beats 42-57 — Focus tools

Focus take, `k` 0-16. Tool library, Cursor halo, Spotlight: "Show your
pointer." gets a cyan halo and ripples on each click. The spotlight walks the
board and each picture is clicked, so the halo ripples; "Spotlight it." sends a
matching ripple on each click. The camera follows the pointer down to the
toolbar: Stop spotlight, then Effects, circled by a hand-drawn ring. It follows
the pointer back up as the blur region is dragged; "Blur" blurs inside a
dashed selection when the real blur lands.

## Beats 57-64 — Cut 3 and lockup

The blur region grows from the tile until the frame is night (beat 57). "Draw
over / any app." builds (beat 58), the ellipse draws (beat 59), the arrow and
wordmark land (beat 60), and the frame holds on the exact picture of frame 0.
