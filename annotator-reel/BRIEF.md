## Intent

The owner's first brief: a world-class motion designer's résumé reel for the
product. Open with a striking hook in the first second, build through distinct
techniques (typography, shape play, camera moves, colour shifts) around the real
interface in use, and land on a clean final frame that loops back to frame 0.
It is the website's main promotional piece, cut to music.

The owner's review of the 15-second cut: "too fast, potentially stretch this to
20 seconds (or even more?), change the crappy music to something way better",
"we don't see much of the tool bar, try to pan over the toolbar so we would see
the different tools there" and "it's really hard to focus". Later notes: a
slower toolbar pan; centre on the colour picker and pause before clicking; a
short pause before choosing the highlighter; a gradient for the Smart Draw
marks; click each picture during the Spotlight so the halo ripple shows;
"fast paced but not like the Flash"; and show the pointer choosing the blur
tool. Sound: the owner rejected generated effects ("too future and robotic,
too many, not related to the content"), so only real foley for visible
actions remains.

This version is 31 seconds (16 bars). It opens on a slow pan along the real
toolbar with labelled tool groups. Actions play at about 1.1x to 1.4x, pointer
travel at up to about 1.8x, with one camera move per action and a hold.

## Customizations

- Cut to music: the reel runs at the song's own tempo (130 BPM; the song is
  129.98) and the music is not stretched. tools/build_audio.py maps reel beats
  to song beats: the reel opens on the last beat of the build, the second drop
  lands on "Draw" (beat 2), one 2-bar edit hides under the zoom-through (beat
  42), and the song's final hit lands on the end card (beat 58) and rings out.
  The Smart Draw snap (beat 34) falls on the drop's second phrase.
- Two renders from one source: index.html (1920x1080) and phone.html
  (1080x1350), generated by tools/make_phone.py only while rendering.
- The website files are silent and loop. Review cuts with music are local only.
- Sound design is foley only, from tools/build_audio.py: one quiet click per
  visible click, one real key per typed character and a soft pen stroke under
  the three hand-drawn strokes. Nothing for titles, camera moves or cuts.
- Tempo lives in tools/shots.py (BPM, FRAMES) and src/reel-lib.js (R.BPM);
  tools/set_timing.py rewrites the clip windows in index.html from beats.
