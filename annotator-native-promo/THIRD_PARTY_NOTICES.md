# Video authoring dependencies

- `assets/gsap.min.js`: GSAP 3.14.2 from the pinned npm package; original
  copyright/license header retained. Standard no-charge license:
  https://gsap.com/standard-license. This is not represented as MIT.
- Broadside design preset and line-by-line-slide authoring primitive:
  HyperFrames workflow/registry, https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes.
  Locally adapted for an original Annotator showcase; not part of the native app.
- Fonts resolve to local macOS Helvetica Neue and Menlo. Font files are not
  bundled or redistributed. Preview/render fidelity is tied to this Mac.
- Native recording and on-screen product copy are original app-owned synthetic
  material. No competitor source, desktop capture or third-party stock is used.
